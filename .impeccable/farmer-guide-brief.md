# Farmer soil and planting workflow

Mode: Operate/Read within the established globe-first surface. Extend the existing
IBM Plex Sans, framed white sheet and quiet teal; no new brand direction is needed.
User has authorised implementation and committing the guided rotation comparison.
Remote pushes and public deployment are outside this feature request.

Audience: farmers and nontechnical advisers returning to the same fields across
seasons. Their questions lead: where is my land, what do these soil numbers mean,
what remains unknown, what crops should I discuss locally, how long might they grow,
what did past seasons look like, and what could future climate change?

New content belongs to the selected region. Remove the economics/calculator route
from the product journey; retain historical backend/research code as archived scope.
Plain definitions sit beside the soil rows; weather graphs explain units, meaning,
missing values and how to read the bars. Additional numerical detail is disclosed.

Navigation adds place search, detailed satellite imagery and road/place labels,
visible zoom controls and optional device location. Fine imagery is navigation,
never a claim of fine soil evidence. Small rectangles may contain only one source cell.

Planting is a transparent FAO pH/temperature screen of seven crops, with indicative
duration, explicit water/nutrient gaps, historical NASA seasons and separate future
NEX model results. No overall suitability probability, yield/harvest-rate estimate,
exact-year weather guarantee or future soil-health score is allowed.

The long-term question about repeated cropping is explicit: current evidence does
not simulate soil degradation. Field notebooks record actual seasons and comparable
laboratory pH observations as user-provided evidence. Save/import/export/undo makes
repeat use possible; browser-only storage and backup limits must be understandable.

Verify the live source flow, crop/season arithmetic, field persistence, missingness,
source failure/recovery, mobile layouts and accessibility. Final visual review uses
actual live-data captures and distinguishes those from synthetic interaction fixtures.
