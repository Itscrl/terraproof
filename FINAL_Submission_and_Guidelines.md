# Official Submission Guidelines

*Participant Reference | Submission Requirements and Procedures*

## 1. Submission Requirements

All participating teams are required to complete **two separate submissions**: the official NASA Space Apps Challenge entry and the local submission for the Legazpi City event. Both submissions are necessary for participation in the local judging process. However, only the official NASA submission constitutes the team's official hackathon entry.

| Submission | What it is | Where it goes |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Official NASA Submission** | The team's official project entry submitted through the NASA Space Apps Challenge website in accordance with NASA's requirements and deadline. | Official NASA submission page. Copy the project link; you will need it for B. |
| **B. Local Event Submission** | Extra materials requested only for our Legazpi City presentation and judging: team details, slides, demo video, and the link to A. | Local Submission Form |

## 2. Official NASA requirements

- All participating teams must ensure that their project's GitHub repository is set to **Public** so that judges and other authorized reviewers can access and review the project's source code.
- Teams must submit an accessible, publicly viewable video link that clearly demonstrates their project's functionality and key features while remaining playable and fully compliant with NASA’s official submission guidelines throughout judging.

## 3. Local submission materials

| Required Material | Purpose | Submission Format |
|---|---|---|
| Official NASA submission link | Allows organizers and judges to access the team's official entry | URL |
| Team and project details | Identifies the team, members and chosen challenge | Local Submission Form |
| Project description | Briefly explains the problem addressed and the proposed solution | Short description, up to 250 words |
| Presentation slides | Supports the live presentation | PDF (preferred) or PPTX |

# Application Demonstration Video Guidelines

*Participant Reference | Requirements for the Project Demonstration Video*

## 1. Purpose

- **Project Evaluation.** Allows judges to observe the application's functionality, features, and implementation.
- **Project Documentation.** Provides a visual record of the solutions developed by participating teams during the event.
- **Project Promotion.** May be used for event-related promotional activities, subject to the team's consent and applicable NASA guidelines.

## 2. Recommended Video Content Structure

| Part | Content | Suggested Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Introduction | Team name and project name | 15 sec |
| 2. Problem | What problem are you addressing? | 30 sec |
| 3. Solution | What does your application do? | 30 sec |
| 4. Live demonstration | Show the actual application working, screen recording with voice-over | 1.5–2 min |
| 5. Features | Explain the main functionalities | 45 sec |
| 6. Impact | How does your solution help? | 30 sec |

The suggested structure serves as a guide for organizing the video presentation. Teams may adjust the sequence and duration of each section, provided that all essential aspects of the project are covered within the prescribed video duration.

## 3. Technical Specifications

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Length | 3–5 minutes |
| Format | MP4, 1080p or 720p |
| Audio | Clear voice-over in English or Filipino with English subtitles encouraged |
| File name | `TeamName_ProjectName_Demo.mp4` |
| Submission | Through the Local Submission Form |
| Deadline | November 15, 2026; 5:00 PM |

## 4. Video Production Guidelines

### Recommended Practices

- Demonstrate the actual working features of the application or prototype.
- Clearly identify any features that are still under development, simulated, or presented as mock-ups.
- Explain how NASA data is incorporated into the application's functionality.
- Ensure that the video, audio, and demonstration links are clear and accessible.
- Review the completed video before submission to check its accuracy and technical quality.

### Restrictions and Precautions

- Do not use copyrighted music, footage, or other materials without the necessary permissions.
- Do not display passwords, API keys, login credentials, or confidential personal information.
- Do not present planned or incomplete features as fully operational functionalities.

## 5. Evaluation of the Demonstration Video

The submitted demonstration video will serve as supplementary material during the judging process. It will allow judges to assess the functionality, usability, and technical implementation of the project, particularly in relation to the Validity and Relevance criteria. The video will also support the evaluation of Presentation by providing additional insight into how the team communicates and demonstrates its solution.
